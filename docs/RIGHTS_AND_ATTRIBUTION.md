# Rights and attribution

Public availability never grants reuse rights. Rights assessments store state, disposition, automatic/manual origin, reviewer/evidence, policy and assessment versions, attribution, restrictions, expiry, geographic restrictions, and platform restrictions. Automatic results can inform review only and cannot satisfy release gates.
# Rights and attribution

Manual upload declarations, attribution, source URL, and notes are unverified source metadata. Possession of uploaded bytes and a successful upload do not establish ownership, licensing, or approval. Existing manual rights and moderation gates remain mandatory.
