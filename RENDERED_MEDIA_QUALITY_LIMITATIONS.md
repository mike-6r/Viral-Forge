# Rendered Media Quality limitations

The local provider is deterministic and bounded. It can verify container/stream evidence and sampled pixel/audio signals, but it does not identify people, use face recognition, retain extracted frames, or guarantee platform acceptance.

OCR is disabled by default. Without it, burned-in subtitle readability, exact text, contrast, line fit, and timing are explicitly marked for human preview review. Safe-area and framing scores are conservative configuration-guided estimates, not semantic subject-recognition claims. Low-motion and near-black samples are advisory and may be intentional.

Retention holds protect the authoritative asset only while inspection is running; a pre-existing hold is preserved. Failed inspection never blocks ordinary clip review and never modifies media.
