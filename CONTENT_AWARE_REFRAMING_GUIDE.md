# Content-aware reframing guide

The correction workflow preserves source attribution and protected evidence overlays. It does not remove, blur, conceal, clone, or crop material to hide watermarks, timestamps, agency branding, or body-camera labels.

No crop control is exposed until existing renderer support and persisted framing evidence can prove the proposed crop is bounded and does not obscure a protected region. Operators should use manual review where framing evidence is insufficient.
